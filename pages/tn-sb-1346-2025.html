TN SB 1346 (2025): AN ACT to amend Tennessee Code Annotated, Title 28 and Title 39, Chapter 17, relative to the unauthorized use of deepfake or actual likeness.

Creates criminal and civil remedies for intentional, nonconsensual disclosure of an intimate digital depiction in covered circumstances.
