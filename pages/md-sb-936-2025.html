MD SB 936 (2025): Consumer Protection - High-Risk Artificial Intelligence - Developer and Deployer Requirements

Would require developers and deployers of high-risk AI systems to exercise reasonable care against foreseeable algorithmic discrimination. It would require impact assessments and disclosures and authorize attorney general enforcement.
