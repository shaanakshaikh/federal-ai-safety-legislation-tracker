H.R. 8170: MATCH Act

Would require U.S. agencies to identify strategically important semiconductor-manufacturing equipment and covered foreign facilities, press allied supplier countries to adopt equivalent export and servicing controls, and extend U.S. controls when allied gaps remain. The bill targets equipment used to produce advanced chips that underpin artificial-intelligence systems and would impose licensing policies of denial for covered destinations and facilities in countries of concern.
