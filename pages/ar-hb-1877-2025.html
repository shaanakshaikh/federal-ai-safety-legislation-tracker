AR HB 1877 (2025): Concerning criminal offenses related to possession of sexually explicit material that depicts a child; and to include a computer generated image that is indistinguishable from the image of a child.

Extends child sexual abuse material laws to cover certain AI-generated depictions. It includes a limited provision for good-faith adversarial testing to prevent or mitigate harmful outputs.
