CA SB 53 (2025): Transparency in Frontier Artificial Intelligence Act

Requires covered large frontier developers to publish safety frameworks, creates critical-safety-incident reporting to the state, protects specified whistleblowers, and establishes CalCompute-related public-compute planning.
