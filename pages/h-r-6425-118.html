H.R. 6425: To direct the Secretary of Defense to establish a working group to develop and coordinate an artificial intelligence initiative among the Five Eyes countries, and for other purposes.

Would establish a Five Eyes working group to evaluate defense AI interoperability, test risks from combined model use and compare ethical frameworks.
