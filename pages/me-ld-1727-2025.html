ME LD 1727 (2025): An Act to Ensure Transparency in Consumer Transactions Involving Artificial Intelligence

Prohibits using a chatbot in consumer transactions in a way that could mislead a reasonable consumer into believing they are dealing with a person unless that fact is disclosed.
