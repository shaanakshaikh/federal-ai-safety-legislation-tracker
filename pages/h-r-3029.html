H.R. 3029: To amend the Research and Development, Competition, and Innovation Act to support nucleic acid screening, and for other purposes.

Directs NIST to develop technical standards and best practices for nucleic-acid synthesis screening and for engineering-biology risks, expressly including risks associated with AI. The House-passed version authorizes $5 million annually for FY2027–FY2031.
