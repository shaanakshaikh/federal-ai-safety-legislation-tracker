H.R. 3029: Nucleic Acid Standards for Biosecurity Act

Establishes a NIST-led consortium and technical standards or best practices for custom synthetic nucleic-acid screening, expressly including risks associated with AI. The reported authorization was $5 million annually for FY2026–FY2030.
