TX SB 815 (2025): Relating to the use of certain automated systems in, and certain adverse determinations made in connection with, the health benefit claims process.

Establishes safeguards for use of automated decision systems in health benefit claims, including restrictions on adverse determinations and requirements for notification or appeal.
