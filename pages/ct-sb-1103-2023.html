CT SB 1103 (2023): An Act Concerning Artificial Intelligence, Automated Decision-Making And Personal Data Privacy

Requires state agencies to assess AI systems for unlawful discrimination and disparate impact before implementing them. Requires an inventory and ongoing review of state AI systems.
