MN HF 1370 (2023): Nonconsensual Dissemination of Deep Fake Sexual Images and Deep Fakes in Elections

Creates criminal penalties and civil remedies for nonconsensual dissemination of intimate deepfakes. Restricts certain deepfakes intended to influence an election.
