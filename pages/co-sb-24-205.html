CO SB24-205: Consumer Protections for Artificial Intelligence

Requires developers and deployers of high-risk AI systems to use reasonable care against known or reasonably foreseeable algorithmic-discrimination risks, publish specified disclosures, complete impact assessments, and report certain discovered risks.
