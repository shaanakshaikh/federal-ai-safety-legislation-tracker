CO SB24-205: Consumer Protections for Artificial Intelligence

Enacted Colorado’s original risk-management, impact-assessment and disclosure framework for high-risk AI. Its requirements were delayed and later repealed and reenacted with changes by SB26-189 before becoming operative.
