IL SB 3444 (2026): Artificial Intelligence Safety Act

Would create a liability safe harbor for frontier-model developers that publish safety and security protocols and transparency reports, with alternative compliance available through qualifying European Union requirements or a federal agreement. The state requirements would sunset if overlapping federal requirements take effect.
