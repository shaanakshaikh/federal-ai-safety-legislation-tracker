H.R. 2683: Remote Access Security Act

Would extend the Export Control Reform Act to remote access by foreign persons—including access through the internet or cloud computing services—when the covered item could pose a serious national-security or foreign-policy risk. The change would let the Commerce Department license and enforce controls on remote access to controlled technology, closing a pathway that can provide foreign users access to advanced computing without a physical export.
