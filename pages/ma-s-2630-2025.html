MA S.2630 (2025): An Act promoting economic development with emerging artificial intelligence models and safety

Would regulate covered frontier models through safety and security protocols, catastrophic-risk controls, incident investigations and reporting, whistleblower protections, and enforcement by the Attorney General. It also establishes duties for large computing clusters and creates an AI innovation fund.
