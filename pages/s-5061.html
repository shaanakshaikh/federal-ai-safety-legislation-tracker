S. 5061: Secure AI Development Act

Establishes secure pre-release testing for frontier AI, modernizes vulnerability reporting, reviews AI vulnerabilities under the federal Vulnerabilities Equities Process, and strengthens NSA AI Security Center and laboratory/system-security functions.
