P.L. 119-12: TAKE IT DOWN Act

Criminalizes specified nonconsensual publication of intimate visual depictions, including AI-created digital forgeries, and requires covered platforms to operate a notice-and-removal process and remove validly reported content and known identical copies within 48 hours.
