TN HB 1513 (2026): AN ACT to amend Tennessee Code Annotated, Title 2, Chapter 19, relative to the Transparency for Deepfakes in Political Advertising Act.

Requires disclosures for political advertising that uses a deepfake to depict a candidate's speech or conduct. It establishes a misdemeanor and allows affected candidates to seek damages and equitable relief.
