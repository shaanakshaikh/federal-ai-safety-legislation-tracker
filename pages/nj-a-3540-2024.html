NJ A 3540 (2024): An Act establishing civil and criminal penalties for the production and dissemination of deceptive audio or visual media

Establishes civil and criminal consequences for specified unauthorized production or disclosure of deceptive audio or visual media and for use of such media in committing certain offenses.
