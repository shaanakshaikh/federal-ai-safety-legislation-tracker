H.R. 5022: No Advanced Chips for the CCP Act of 2025.

Would bar exports, reexports, or transfers of covered advanced AI semiconductors to China unless the Commerce Secretary approves them after an interagency national-security review and Congress separately enacts a joint resolution of approval. The authority would sunset after three years.
