KS HB 2313 (2025): Prohibiting the use of the artificial intelligence platform DeepSeek and other artificial intelligence platforms controlled by a country of concern on state-owned devices and on any state network and the use of genetic sequencers or operational software used for genetic analysis that is produced in a foreign adversary

Bars Kansas state agencies from accessing DeepSeek or AI platforms controlled by listed countries of concern on state devices and networks. Exempts specified law enforcement and cybersecurity investigations.
