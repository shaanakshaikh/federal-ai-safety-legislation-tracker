S. 4656: Secure and Accountable Military AI Act of 2026

Defines covered military AI security incidents to include model-weight theft or exfiltration, foreign influence, supply-chain compromise, data poisoning or backdoors, and concerning behavior involving offensive cyber capabilities, safeguard evasion, or chemical and biological weapons.
