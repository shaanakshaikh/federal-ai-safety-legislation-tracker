S. 2676: Cloud LAB Act of 2025

Would establish an NSF cloud-laboratory network that generates biological data for AI training and other research. Its implementation plan must build in cybersecurity, biosecurity, and research security, while an advisory board would address misuse safeguards and rigorous cybersecurity across the network.
