P.L. 118-159 §§ 225, 236, 1531–1534, 6504: Servicemember Quality of Life Improvement and National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2025

The FY2025 NDAA strengthened protection of advanced AI from adversary access, launched defense AI-and-biotechnology work, improved human-factors and evaluation infrastructure for military AI, and established the NSA Artificial Intelligence Security Center.
