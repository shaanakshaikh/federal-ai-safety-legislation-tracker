P.L. 118-159 §§ 225, 236, 1531–1534, 6504: FY2025 NDAA artificial-intelligence security, biosecurity, and assurance provisions

A curated group of FY2025 NDAA provisions directing assessment and protection of advanced AI from adversary access, piloting national-security AI and biotechnology applications, strengthening human-factors and testing infrastructure for military AI, and establishing the NSA Artificial Intelligence Security Center.
