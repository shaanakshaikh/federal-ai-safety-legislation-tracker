P.L. 118-159 § 225: Defense governing-council duties for advanced AI security

Directs the Chief Digital and Artificial Intelligence Officer Governing Council to identify and assess AI models that could pose national-security risk if accessed by an adversary, develop strategies against unauthorized adversary access and use, and recommend legislative or administrative action.
