IL HB 3773 (2023): Illinois Human Rights Act — Artificial Intelligence in Employment

Prohibits employers from using AI in specified employment decisions if it has a discriminatory effect or uses ZIP codes as a proxy for protected classes. Requires notice when covered employment decisions use AI.
