P.L. 119-74 § 4: Explanatory statement

Gives the explanatory statement effect for fund allocation and implementation. The Division A statement specifies at least $55 million for ongoing NIST AI research and measurement, including up to $10 million for the Center for AI Standards and Innovation, and adopts direction for AI model evaluations.
