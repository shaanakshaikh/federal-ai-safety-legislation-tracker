KY SB 4 (2025): AN ACT relating to protection of information and declaring an emergency.

Establishes an AI governance committee and requires Kentucky to set procedures for developing, approving, and using AI in government, including safeguards for sensitive data and high-risk uses. It also creates a civil remedy for covered synthetic-media election communications, subject to a disclosure defense.
