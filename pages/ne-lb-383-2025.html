NE LB 383 (2025): Adopt the Parental Rights in Social Media Act, rename the Child Pornography Prevention Act, and prohibit conduct involving computer-generated child pornography

Extends Nebraska child sexual abuse material laws to specified computer-generated depictions and prohibits their production and distribution. Incorporates provisions from LB 172.
