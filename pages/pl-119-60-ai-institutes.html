P.L. 119-60 §§ 224, 245, 1512–1515, 1533–1535, 6601–6604: FY2026 NDAA artificial-intelligence security and oversight provisions

A curated group of FY2026 NDAA provisions covering secure biological data for AI, Defense-wide AI cybersecurity policy, physical and supply-chain security requirements for AI contractors, model assessment and secure sandboxes, frontier-AI governance and human-override safeguards, NSA security guidance against nation-state theft, and intelligence-community testing and performance oversight.
