P.L. 119-60 §§ 224, 245, 563, 1061, 1512–1515, 1532–1535, 3117, 6601–6604: National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2026

The FY2026 NDAA established new defense and intelligence safeguards for national-security AI research, biological data, model and supply-chain security, testing and oversight, frontier-AI planning, and intelligence-community use of public models.
