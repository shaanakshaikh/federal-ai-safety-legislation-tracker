H.R. 8845: Commerce, Justice, Science, and Related Agencies Appropriations Act, 2027

The House-reported FY2027 CJS bill and report provide up to $15 million for the Center for AI Standards and Innovation, including a hardware-security and secure-location-verification grant program. The report also directs work on an AI lifecycle-security framework, open-source model security risks, small-business AI resources, and at least $30 million for advancement of the National AI Research Resource.
