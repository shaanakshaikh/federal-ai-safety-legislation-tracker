S. 5239: AWARE Act of 2024

Would have required the Department of Defense to maintain a ledger of covered AI systems and conduct recurring risk assessments addressing dependability, cybersecurity, privacy, bias, escalation, swarm deployment, civilian harm, model updates, and exports to foreign users.
