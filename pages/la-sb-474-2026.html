LA SB 474 (2026): Protecting Louisiana’s Infrastructure from Artificial Intelligence Risk Act

Would require covered frontier-model developers to disclose their safety frameworks and address catastrophic risks, including risks to critical infrastructure. It provides for reporting critical safety incidents and protects covered whistleblowers.
