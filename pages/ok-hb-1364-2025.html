OK HB 1364 (2025): Crimes and punishments; sexual obscenity; making certain acts unlawful; effective date.

Expands Oklahoma’s intimate-image law to cover specified artificially generated sexual depictions, including AI-generated images, and adds penalties and a removal remedy.
