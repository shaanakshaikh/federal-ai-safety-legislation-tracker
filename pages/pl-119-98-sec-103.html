P.L. 119-98 § 103: Border security, technology, and screening

Funds CBP border-security and screening capabilities, including AI and machine-learning inspection equipment. Bars use of this section’s funds to buy or deploy southwest and northern border surveillance towers until CBP has tested and accepted their autonomous capabilities.
