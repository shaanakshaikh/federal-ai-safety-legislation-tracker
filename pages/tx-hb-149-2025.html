TX HB 149 (2025): Texas Responsible Artificial Intelligence Governance Act

Creates a statewide AI governance framework with disclosure duties for specified governmental and health-care uses, prohibitions on certain harmful or unlawful development and deployment, biometric protections, civil penalties, and a state regulatory sandbox.
