H.R. 9566: To establish a pilot program for use by U.S. Customs and Border Protection at land ports of entry along the Arizona border to assess the use of artificial intelligence through an anomaly detection algorithm, and for other purposes.

Would require a CBP pilot of AI-assisted border screening, including testing accuracy and false alarms, applying DHS cybersecurity requirements, and managing cyber risks on an ongoing basis.
