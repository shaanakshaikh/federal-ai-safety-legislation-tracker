NE LB 525 (2025): Agricultural Data Privacy Act and Conversational Artificial Intelligence Safety Act

Enacts conversational AI protections including disclosures, measures against harmful interactions with children, account controls, and a suicide and self-harm response protocol. Provisions from LB 1185 were incorporated through AM 2444.
