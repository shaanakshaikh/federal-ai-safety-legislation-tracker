DE HB 316 (2024): An Act to Amend Title 15 of the Delaware Code Relating to Deep Fakes in Elections

Prohibits certain distribution of deceptive election deepfakes within 90 days of an election, subject to disclosure and other exceptions, and provides penalties and injunctive relief.
