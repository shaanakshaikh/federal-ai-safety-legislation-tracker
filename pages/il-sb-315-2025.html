IL SB 315 (2025): Artificial Intelligence Safety Measures Act

Requires large frontier developers to maintain and publish frontier-AI safety frameworks, publish transparency reports, assess catastrophic risks, undergo annual independent audits, report critical safety incidents, and protect covered whistleblowers. The Illinois Emergency Management Agency and Office of Homeland Security administer reporting and guidance in consultation with the Attorney General.
