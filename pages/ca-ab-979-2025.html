CA AB 979 (2025): California Cybersecurity Integration Center: artificial intelligence

Directs the California Cybersecurity Integration Center to develop an AI Cybersecurity Collaboration Playbook and strengthen sharing of AI-related threat, vulnerability, and incident information.
