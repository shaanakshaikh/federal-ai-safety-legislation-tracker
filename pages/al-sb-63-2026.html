AL SB 63 (2026): Health care plans; to regulate the use of artificial intelligence in determinations of coverage

Restricts health plan use of AI in prior-authorization decisions and requires consideration of the enrollee’s medical history and clinical circumstances. It authorizes state insurance enforcement.
