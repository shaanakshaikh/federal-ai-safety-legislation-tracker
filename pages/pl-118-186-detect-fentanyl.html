P.L. 118-186: DETECT Fentanyl and Xylazine Act of 2024

Authorizes DHS research, testing, evaluation, and cost-benefit analysis for AI- and machine-learning-enabled tools that identify fentanyl, xylazine, controlled-substance analogues, and emerging psychoactive substances, while requiring the work to follow the NIST AI Risk Management Framework.
