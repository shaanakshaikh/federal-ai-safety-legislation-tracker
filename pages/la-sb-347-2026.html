LA SB 347 (2026): Provides for the crime of "unlawful deepfake" to be added to the definition of power-based violence under the Campus Accountability and Safety Act

Adds unlawful deepfakes to the definition of power-based violence for the Campus Accountability and Safety Act, bringing this conduct within the Act's institutional response requirements.
