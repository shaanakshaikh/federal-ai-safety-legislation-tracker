MA H.97 (2025): An Act protecting consumers in interactions with artificial intelligence systems

Would require developers and deployers of high-risk AI systems used in consequential decisions to manage discrimination risks, perform impact assessments, make disclosures, notify affected consumers, and provide explanation and appeal processes, with enforcement by the Attorney General.
