H.R. 5272: Protect Elections from Deceptive AI Act

Would prohibit knowing distribution of materially deceptive AI-generated audio or visual media involving federal candidates, or used in federal election activity, when intended to influence an election or solicit funds, subject to specified news, satire, and other exceptions.
