P.L. 119-60 § 8521: Prohibition and notification on investments relating to covered national security transactions

Adds Title VIII to the Defense Production Act, authorizing Treasury to prohibit or require notification of certain outbound investments involving covered foreign persons and technologies, including specified AI systems and semiconductors.
