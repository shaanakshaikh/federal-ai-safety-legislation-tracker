S. 4615: Intelligence Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2027

Title VII, sections 701–711, addresses AI exploitation and intelligence sharing, targeting review, testing standards, incident tracking, agentic-system access controls, AI Security Center duties, targeting-output labeling, escalation research, adversarial-AI interaction, proliferation and export assessments, the Vulnerabilities Equities Process, and prohibited models.
