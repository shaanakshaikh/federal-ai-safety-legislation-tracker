IA SF 2417 (2026): Conversational Artificial Intelligence Services — Limitations, Required Disclosures, and Civil Penalties

Requires operators of conversational AI services to implement child safeguards, disclosures, account controls, and suicide or self-harm response protocols. The requirements apply beginning July 1, 2027.
