IL HB 1806 (2025): Wellness and Oversight for Psychological Resources Act

Reserves therapy and psychotherapy for licensed professionals. Limits AI use to permitted support functions and prohibits AI from independently making therapeutic decisions or directly interacting with clients for therapeutic communication.
