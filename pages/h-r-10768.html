H.R. 10768: To establish the Office of Artificial Intelligence and Emerging Technology Threats within the Executive Office of the President, and for other purposes.

Would establish an Executive Office of the President office to advise the President on AI and emerging-technology threats, coordinate federal agencies, and monitor risks and opportunities.
